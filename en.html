      In TÜBİTAK’s terms

      Where do the ideas count in the TÜBİTAK index?

      TÜBİTAK’s Entrepreneurial and Innovative University Index measures universities on four dimensions with 23 indicators. Each idea whose output can be counted directly under an indicator is mapped to it. Teknokent community ideas are not mapped, as their contribution is indirect. Tap an indicator to filter the shelf by the ideas that feed it.

      31%

      Almost a third of the index comes from four indicators: the number and net sales of academic and student/graduate companies in technoparks, TEKMERs and incubation centres. This is where Teknokent contributes to the university most directly.

      Source: TÜBİTAK, Entrepreneurial and Innovative University Index, 2024 indicator set and weights (announced 28 May 2025). The mapping is a suggestion; it will change as we talk with the units.
